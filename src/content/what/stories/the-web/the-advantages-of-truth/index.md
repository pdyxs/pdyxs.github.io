If we want to combat disinformation and misinformation, we have to ask ourselves: what are the strengths of well-researched, evidence-backed information, when it comes to communication? What do we need to surface to ensure that it comes out on top?

The answer: the strength of something backed by evidence (whether that be scientific evidence or otherwise) is our ability to continue to ask questions and to get reasonable answers. The sheer depth of those answers is where the power lies.

In other words - the thing that makes a piece of information worthwhile has nothing to do with the surface of that information - it's in the depth of the . It's in my ability to look at something that doesn't make sense to me (because let's face it, the surface level of truth often looks pretty bonkers), ask questions about it, and get an answer that, at some level (maybe after a lot more questions) makes sense.

Yet in the modern day, we're trending towards mediums that focus less and less on that depth, and more on the surface.
