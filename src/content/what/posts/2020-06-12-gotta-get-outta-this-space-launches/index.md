Gotta Get Outta This Space launches tomorrow!

These crew cards are, in many ways, the most important cards in the game, as they let you be part of the story of the game.

Each player's goal is to pick themselves up from a planet, and when they do, they join the crew. Each crew member has powerful one shot powers, and will impact the game from then on.
