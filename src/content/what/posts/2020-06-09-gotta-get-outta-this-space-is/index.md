Gotta Get Outta This Space is launching this Sunday!

These robots are the primary antagonist of Gotta Get Outta This Space. Every turn, they multiply and spread out, slowly but surely covering the board and cutting off your escape. You must act quickly to get what you need before getting out.

One of the inspirations for this game is Conway's Game of Life: a simple set of rules that creates incredible lifelike animations. The robots and their mechanics are the clearest analog of these ideas.
