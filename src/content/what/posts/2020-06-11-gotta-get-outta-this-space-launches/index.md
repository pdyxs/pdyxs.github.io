Gotta Get Outta This Space launches in 2 days!

Your ship is made up of modules, each of which lives on a card like this. As your modules are damaged and upgraded, you'll be updating these cards. You'll also be able to get new modules, which you'll draw yourself and add to your ship.

The main design imperatives for these module cards is simplicity and - that it must be easy for players to fully understand what they can do from the cards themselves, and that they can be altered in creative ways. That's key to making sure that each person playing to game can quickly get into it, while still allowing these ships to evolve and change.
