Today I mocked up an app that would let me own a generic set of Augmented Reality enabled cards  and turn them into any cards I like (in this case skull and roses) by viewing them through my phone. 
There's some pretty big usability issues (holding a hand of cards is next to impossible being the biggest), but if I can solve them I'll be able to prototype games without having to print and cut every time. 
I'm also pretty excited about the possibilities for building games designed for ar: you could give different people different information on the same card or token, which opens up a world of possibilities #boardgamedesign #prototyping #games
