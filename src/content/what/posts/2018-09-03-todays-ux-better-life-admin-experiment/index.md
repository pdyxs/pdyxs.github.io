Today’s UX/better life admin experiment: better transaction sorting.

Many budgeting apps need you to sort your transactions, but I’ve not found any that make transaction sorting simple. Important features here are:
* Connecting to email, so you can link transactions to emails for much-needed context (bonus: you get receipts for free!)
* Automatically detecting potential transfers and fees
* A really simple UI that breaks down categorising your transactions into small tasks. This means I can now do a bit of this life admin whenever I have a few minutes spare, rather than having it be a big block of work
