Attempt #4 of my Visualising Genetic Sequences project. Follow along and get updates at https://medium.com/series/visualising-genetic-sequences-bea25751f4b7⠀
⠀
I've added the amino acids that codons (groups of 3 nucleotides) actually represent. Each of the 21 amino acids that can be built has a slightly different hue (from green to yellow). To build a protein based on a gene, the amino acids are simply joined together in the order specified in the sequence.⠀
⠀
There’s some issues with spacing (some of the genes are too close together and their codon representations are overlapping). I think I’m going to have to adjust the visualisation approach for next time.⠀
