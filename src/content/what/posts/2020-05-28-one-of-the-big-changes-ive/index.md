One of the big changes I’ve had to make to ‘Gotta Get Outta This Space’ in the last few days is the game length - early testers said that they completed the 2 levels given to them and felt that they needed a third to really feel like they were ready to send their spaceship on.

So now there’s a 3 level structure, complete with a map!

I also had to rejig the story a bit (the ‘training mission->real mission’ story didn’t really work with 3 levels) to make it all work - you can see the current version of the first bit of story here.
