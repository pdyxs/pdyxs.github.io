Only the electromagnetic force is on. Charged particles push and pull on each other; colourless, neutral and coloured particles alike ignore everything but charge. Your particle is a down quark (negative), so electrons repel it and positrons pull it in. Steer with the arrow keys, WASD, or by holding a pointer.
