I'm Paul Sztajer, a software engineer and interaction designer. My favourite thing to do is to develop works that illuminate complex systems and ideas.

I studied Physics and Software Engineering at University and then dived into the world of games and education. Along the way, I started [a game studio](card:where/work/seethrough), worked on [science and maths games used in schools](tag:education), have designed [interactive theatre](card:what/games/analog/art-heist), and more.
